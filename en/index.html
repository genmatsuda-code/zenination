The Goodwill Nation ── a founding teaser

積善

Good does not vanish. It compounds as social capital.

One person's good deed is inscribed as a proof — an unforgeable record. For every deed inscribed, the nation gains capital, and a door opens — a seat, a room, a tie to another person. 積善 (SEKIZEN, accumulated good). A house that accumulates good is sure to have blessings to spare.

The three phases ── the arc

From a village, to a nation.
Growth follows an order.

We do not raise a nation overnight. First we build a small, certain village, and its people call the next. Once we pass 10,000, the growth carries itself. This is the threshold.

The reflux of surplus blessing ── the house of accumulated good

A house that accumulates good
is sure to have blessings to spare.

This is not an ornament of words — it is the very mechanism of this nation. The citizens you invited, and the citizens they invited — the lineage that begins with you is called a "House." The good your House's citizens accumulate returns to its Founder as merit.

The reflux has a ceiling. Merit gained from surplus blessing may be at most 49% of a person's rank. More than half must always come from one's own good deeds. ── So in this nation, "invite the good and raise them well" is rewarded more than "invite many." Those who chase numbers alone can never rise.

What returns is merit, not money. Not a structure that siphons upward (money rising from below), but one that reflects back (the good below reflected into the merit above). This is the single point on which this nation differs from every other community.
