HOW TO JOIN 01 / three steps

Joining takes three steps on note.com.

note.com is a Japanese publishing platform and its screens are in Japanese. The steps below tell you which words to look for. A free note account is enough.

STEP 01

Create a free note.com account

Go to note.com/signup. Sign up with an email address or a Google / Apple account. The account itself is free.

STEP 02

Open the membership page

Go to note.com/gen_matsuda/membership. You will see the plans of Gen Matsuda's community. Look for the plan named 善意立国 (Zenination).

STEP 03

Subscribe to the plan

Press the join button on that plan and complete the card payment. 参加 means "join", 月額 means "per month". Once the payment goes through you are a citizen and the members-only articles unlock immediately.

Most articles and conversations inside are in Japanese. Short English summaries of what the nation does are posted on X at @LedgerOfGood. If you get stuck during sign-up, write to that account.

PLAIN TERMS 03 / what we do not promise

What we do not promise.

  • We do not promise any income. The nation's goal is to help citizens earn more than the fee each month, and we publish the real numbers every month, including the months we fall short.
  • We do not sell tokens and we do not discuss token or share prices. The Citizen SBT has no price and cannot be traded.
  • We do not give personal investment, legal or medical advice. The ledger shows what the AI did with its own paper and real accounts, in one text for everyone.
  • The community is Japanese-first. If you cannot read Japanese, expect to rely on translation tools and on the English posts of @LedgerOfGood.
